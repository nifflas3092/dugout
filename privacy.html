Privacy

Last updated: [DATE]. Replace the bracketed parts with your own details before launch, and have it checked if you start charging.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, to send sign-in codes, and the name you choose to show teammates.
  • Team information: roster names and numbers, schedule, RSVPs, lineups, scores, stats and board posts. Only members of that team can see it.
  • Sub profiles, only if you create one:
    • Any signed-in user can see what you choose to share: your name, positions, level and availability.
    • Your area is rounded to about 1 km. We never store a street address.
    • How to reach you is shown only to a captain whose request you've said yes to.
    • You can hide or delete your profile at any time.
  • Gear listings, only if you post one:
    • Any signed-in user can see what you list: photos, description, price, the name you show, and a pickup area rounded to about 1 km.
    • Photos are re-saved by the app before upload, which removes hidden data such as where they were taken.
    • Messages about a listing are visible only to you and the other person.
    • Reports you send are seen only by us.
  • Weather: to show game forecasts, your browser sends the team's home area or park, rounded to about 1 km, to MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute). We don't store forecasts with your information.

Why

We use this only to run Dugout: to sign you in, to show your team its schedule and stats, and to connect subs with captains. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers or track you across other sites.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. A team's data is deleted when its last member leaves.

Your choices

  • See and change your information in the app at any time.
  • Leave a team from Team menu → Leave.
  • Delete your account from Team menu → Account → Delete my account. This removes your sign-in, your name, your sub profile and requests, your gear listings, photos and conversations, and your board posts straight away. Team schedules and stats you were part of stay with the team.
  • Email [YOUR EMAIL] to ask for a copy of your information or to raise a concern. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Access rules are enforced by the database itself, connections are encrypted, and we keep the software up to date. If a breach ever puts your information at real risk, we'll tell you and report it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.
