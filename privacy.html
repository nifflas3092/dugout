Privacy

Last updated: [DATE]. Replace the bracketed parts with your own details before launch, and have it checked if you start charging.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address (to send sign-in links) and the name you choose to show teammates.
  • Team information captains enter: roster names and numbers, schedule, RSVPs, lineups, scores, stats and board posts. Only members of that team can see it.
  • Sub profiles, only if you create one: what you choose to share (name, positions, level, availability, how to reach you) and your rough area. The area is rounded to about 1 km; we never store a street address. Signed-in captains can see sub profiles. You can hide or delete yours at any time.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information or show ads based on it. We don't track you across other sites.

Who processes it
