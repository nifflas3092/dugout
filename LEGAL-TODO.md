# Legal to-do (saved Nov 5, 2026, not done yet)

Notes to act on before launch. Have a Canadian lawyer review the final wording.

## 1. Terms of Service and liability waivers (Gear marketplace)
- **"As-is" transactions:** Dugout is only a venue for communication. It does not inspect, guarantee or warrant any equipment sold (cracked bats, altered gear, expired or missing certification stamps such as USSSA/ASA).
- **Limitation of liability:** not liable for physical injury from gear bought on the platform, financial loss, or fraudulent transactions.
- **Prohibited items:** illegal, altered, stolen or non-approved equipment.

## 2. Privacy Policy and PIPEDA
Users create accounts, post contact details and send messages, so PIPEDA applies. The policy must say clearly:
- what data is collected (email, rough location, IP address, …)
- how it's stored and protected
- that user data is not sold to third parties without consent

(`privacy.html` exists but has bracketed parts to fill in, and needs updating for payments, team chat, "online now", the AI schedule import and Gear admin.)

## 3. Intellectual property and brand names
- **Trademarks:** don't use official league or manufacturer logos (USSSA, Softball Canada, Worth, Miken, …) in the app's permanent branding or App Store graphics without permission.
- **User content:** the terms should give Dugout a licence to display photos users post, and make users responsible for not uploading copyrighted images that aren't theirs.

## 4. Banned equipment compliance
Associations (Softball Canada, USSSA, NSA) update their banned bat lists often. Dugout isn't responsible for enforcing league rules, but a disclaimer reminding players to check their own league's rules before buying helps prevent buyer and seller disputes. (Gear already shows a short reminder on bat listings.)
